Lake Life

Discover life around Lake Atitlán

Lake Atitlán is more than a destination. It is a collection of villages, families, markets, trails, gardens, changing weather, and everyday routines shaped by the lake and the volcanoes.

Here you will find observations and practical information about life in the area—its culture and communities, its natural beauty, and what it can feel like to spend time here or make a home.

About Katherine

Twenty-five years of life in Guatemala

My name is Katherine Bird. I have lived in San Marcos La Laguna, Guatemala, since 2001. I arrived without knowing exactly what I would find, and life here became a defining part of my journey.

Over the years, I have developed a deep appreciation for the people, landscapes, culture, slower rhythms, and possibilities of life around Lake Atitlán. I have also spent many years working in holistic practices and exploring practical ways to reconnect with ourselves and what matters.

From Now On brings these interests together: the places we inhabit, the ways we care for ourselves, the ideas we explore, and the possibilities we create.
